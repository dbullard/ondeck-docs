Relay connects to the software and hardware around your production so operators can see everything in one place and act without constant app switching.

Software connections are configured under `Relay > Settings` → <Icon icon="link" /> **External Connections**. Hardware devices are managed under `Relay > Settings` → <Icon icon="hard-drive" /> **External Devices**.

The Setup Wizard is a guided first-run flow that gets you to a working dashboard without building everything by hand.

## What it does

- **Finds your devices** on the network, including OSC-capable devices.
- **Connects your services**, such as Planning Center and ProPresenter.
- **Suggests starter tiles** based on what it found.
- **Builds your first dashboard** with a live layout preview, so you can see the result before you accept it.

Free and Pro users each get their own sequence of steps, so you only see options that apply to your license.

## Reopen the wizard

You can run the wizard again at any time, for example after you add new devices or services:

- **Mac:** choose it from the `File` menu.
- **iOS (Coming Soon):** choose it from the ellipsis menu.

## After the wizard

- Fine-tune your layout with [Tiles](/dashboards/tiles).
- Add more [modules](/dashboards/modules) as your needs grow.
- Review [Core concepts](/core-concepts) if any terms are new.
- If a device was not found, add it manually under [Integrations](/integrations/overview).

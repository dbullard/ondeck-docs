The Setup Wizard walks you through connecting your services, choosing starter tiles, and creating a first dashboard from what you set up. Every step is optional, so you can skip anything you don't use.

## Open the wizard

Choose `File > Setup Wizard…`. You can run it again at any time, for example after you add new devices or services. On iOS (Coming Soon), use the ellipsis menu.

Free and Pro users see their own sequence of steps.

## The steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Welcome">
    The first screen previews what the wizard covers: connecting services, finding devices on your network, and building your first layout. Choose `Get Started` to begin, or `Skip Setup` to close the wizard.

    <Frame>
      ![Setup Wizard welcome screen listing Connect Services, Find Devices, and Build Your Dashboard](/images/screenshots/wizard-1-welcome.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your services">
    Link the services you use. Planning Center syncs service plans and schedules. ProPresenter controls slides and media playback and requires Relay Pro.

    <Frame>
      ![Connect Your Services step showing Planning Center and ProPresenter](/images/screenshots/wizard-2-services.png)
    </Frame>

    Choose `Skip this step` if you don't use either.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose starter tiles">
    Tick the tiles you want, such as PCO Rundown, PCO Team Assignments, Roster Team Assignments, Person, Text, Image, Picker, Document, Webpage, or Time. A live layout preview on the right updates as you choose, so you can see how the tiles will sit on the canvas before you continue.

    <Frame>
      ![Choose Starter Tiles step with Text and Time selected and a layout preview](/images/screenshots/wizard-3-tiles-preview.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and create your dashboard">
    Name your dashboard (the default is "Dashboard 1"), review the tiles, and choose `Create Dashboard`. Wireless devices Relay knows about are listed here as wireless mic and IEM status tiles, each with its own checkbox, so you can leave out any you don't want.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use `Back` on any step to change an earlier choice.

## After the wizard

- Fine-tune your layout with [Tiles](/dashboards/tiles).
- Add more [modules](/dashboards/modules) as your needs grow.
- Review [Core concepts](/core-concepts) if any terms are new.
- If a device wasn't found, add it manually under [Integrations](/integrations/overview).

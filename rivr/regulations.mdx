Open **Regulations** from a location's detail view to see the state or fisheries source associated with that water.

Rivr may show a location-specific summary, statewide defaults, or a notice that the reach has not yet been curated. It can also provide links to the official governing body and fisheries information.

## Always confirm the current rule

Fishing regulations can change by season, date, water reach, species, method, and possession limit. Before fishing, open the official source from Rivr and confirm the rule that applies to your exact location and date.

If Rivr has not verified a location-specific rule, treat that as a prompt to check the state's wildlife agency—not as permission to fish under a default rule.

<Warning>Rivr is not a government agency and is not affiliated with or endorsed by USGS, USACE, SWPA, or state wildlife agencies. Official regulations and posted safety notices control.</Warning>

---
title: "Data Sources & Attributions"
description: "Where Rivr's data comes from, and the credit each source requires."
---

Rivr is independent. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of these organizations.

| Source | What Rivr uses | Terms |
|---|---|---|
| Apple Weather | Current weather and forecasts | [Apple Weather data sources](https://developer.apple.com/weatherkit/data-source-attribution/) |
| U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) — Water Data APIs, NWIS | Streamflow, stage, water temperature | [USGS data licensing](https://www.usgs.gov/data-management/data-licensing) (public domain; credit requested) |
| USGS NLDI / NHDPlus | River network and gauge relationships | [NLDI](https://api.water.usgs.gov/nldi/) |
| USGS National Map — Watershed Boundary Dataset | Matches a location to its watershed for species lookups | [Watershed Boundary Dataset](https://www.usgs.gov/national-hydrography/watershed-boundary-dataset) |
| USGS Aquatic Gap Analysis Project | Modeled fish distribution | [doi:10.5066/P1UV25FW](https://doi.org/10.5066/P1UV25FW) |
| USGS Nonindigenous Aquatic Species | Species occurrence | [nas.er.usgs.gov](https://nas.er.usgs.gov) |
| U.S. Army Corps of Engineers — CWMS and water portals | Dam releases and lake levels | [water.usace.army.mil](https://water.usace.army.mil/help) |
| Tennessee Valley Authority | Dam releases and lake levels | [tva.com](https://www.tva.com) |
| Southwestern Power Administration | Dam generation schedules | [energy.gov/swpa](https://www.energy.gov/swpa) |
| State fish and wildlife agencies | Fishing regulations and stocking | Each agency's published terms; linked from every regulation in the app |
| iNaturalist | Species observations | [iNaturalist terms](https://www.inaturalist.org/terms); individual observations are under their observers' licenses |
| GBIF | Species occurrence records | [GBIF data licences](https://www.gbif.org/terms/licences) (CC0 / CC BY / CC BY-NC) |
| OpenStreetMap | River course geometry | © OpenStreetMap contributors, [ODbL](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) |
| U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder | Matching a location to its state agency | This product uses the Census Bureau Data API but is not endorsed or certified by the Census Bureau. |
| Apple Maps | Maps | Shown on the map itself |

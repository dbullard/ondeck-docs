The detail view's **Rig Advice** uses the available water, weather, hatch, species, and personal preference context to suggest a practical starting setup.

<div className="rivr-field-note"><strong>Start light</strong><br />Treat every recommendation as a first cast, not a fixed answer. Let clarity, light, flow, and fish behavior move you through the game plan.</div>

## What the recommendation includes

You may see:

- Target species and technique
- Rod and line guidance
- Leader, depth, weight, or lure guidance
- Suggested patterns or flies
- A short **AI Guide Briefing** when the optional on-device enhancement is available
- A **Guide's Game Plan** with steps such as **Start here**, **If it is quiet**, and **Read the water**

Recommendations are deliberately framed as starting points. They should change as water clarity, light, flow, insect activity, and fish behavior change.

## When conditions are uncertain

Rivr may show a partial recommendation when a data source is missing or stale. A missing recommendation is preferable to false precision. Check the detail view's reason text and use the most recent observations available at the water.

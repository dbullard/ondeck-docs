---
title: Condition alerts
description: Configure notifications when a saved location changes.
---

Rivr can notify you when a saved location crosses a condition you care about. Open **Settings → Notifications & Alerts** to configure a saved location.

## Alert types

Depending on the location's available data, you can configure alerts for:

- A percentage change in flow
- A CFS change
- A flow level
- A condition-score threshold
- A larger scheduled release later in the day

Not every alert type is available for every location.

## Notification frequency

Choose how often Rivr checks for alert conditions. Available intervals include every hour, two hours, four hours, and eight hours.

Rivr sends a notification when a condition is reached rather than repeatedly notifying you for the same unchanged reading.

## Test and troubleshoot alerts

Use **Send Test Notification** in the alert settings to verify that notifications are allowed. If the test does not appear, check iOS notification permissions for Rivr and confirm that the location has been saved before configuring its alerts.

<Warning>Alerts are not emergency warnings. River levels can change faster than a notification can arrive. Follow official emergency and dam-release notices.</Warning>

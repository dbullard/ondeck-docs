title: Settings and appearance

---

Open **Settings** to control Rivr's appearance and behavior.

## Display & Interface

Choose light, dark, or system appearance. You can also choose among Rivr's visual themes and adjust dashboard card and water-animation preferences.

## Tackle & Techniques

Choose your preferred fishing method. Rivr uses that preference to prioritize recommendations when the available conditions support more than one approach.

## Storage & Caches

Rivr keeps local station directories, coordinates, telemetry snapshots, and dashboard data so it can open quickly and behave well when connectivity is limited. Clearing caches removes those local copies; Rivr rebuilds them the next time it needs them.

## About Rivr

The About and Data Sources screens explain the public telemetry sources Rivr uses, including USGS NWIS and USACE CWMS, along with the important limitations of those readings.

## Search

Tap the location search control and enter part of a river, lake, dam, or gauge name. Rivr searches its catalog of supported locations and can use your current area to help narrow the results.

If a location does not appear, try a shorter name or search for the nearby dam or gauge instead.

## Preview before saving

Open a result to preview its location, water type, available telemetry, and any known regulation source. Some locations have more data than others; a missing metric does not necessarily mean the water is unavailable.

## Save and organize locations

Tap **Add location** to put the water on your dashboard. From **Edit Locations**, drag saved locations into your preferred order or remove locations you no longer follow.

Rivr stores your saved locations on the device and can synchronize them when account sync is available.

<Tip>For a new fishing trip, save the exact reach or tailwater you plan to fish when possible. Conditions and regulations can differ significantly between nearby reaches.</Tip>

Starting with Relay `1.3.0`, **Relay is free and every feature is unlocked for everyone.** There is no license key to enter, no subscription to manage, and no account to sign in to. Everything that used to need Relay Pro, including External Devices, External Control, Synced Tabs, tab schedules, and Automation View, works as soon as you install the update.

<Note>
  Relay is also moving to open source. The source code will be published on GitHub under the MIT license starting with Relay `2.0`. Relay `1.x` stays closed source.
</Note>

## What changed in 1.3

- **Relay Pro is unlocked for everyone.** Settings pages and features that used to show as locked are available.
- **No license checks.** Relay no longer checks a license when it starts or when you switch back to the app, and it works offline.
- **The Subscription page is now a Support page.** Open `Relay > Settings` → <Icon icon="heart" /> **Support** to see it.
- **Relay Account sign-in is being retired.** See below.

If a feature still looks locked, make sure you've installed Relay `1.3.0` or later with `Relay > Check for Updates...`.

## Supporting Relay

If Relay helps your team, you can optionally support its development from `Relay > Settings` → <Icon icon="heart" /> **Support**. The link opens Buy Me a Coffee. Support is voluntary and **never unlocks anything**.

## If you used to pay for Relay Pro

- Your Relay Pro subscription has been cancelled and won't renew, and you don't need to do anything.
- You can ask for a refund of the unused part of what you paid until the end of November 30, 2026. See the [refund terms](/relay/terms-conditions#refunds) or email `support@ondeckapps.com`.
- Licenses and subscriptions from earlier versions no longer matter, so you can stop using any license key you saved.

## Relay Account sign-in is being retired

Relay Account (the email magic-link sign-in) existed to link licenses and subscriptions across devices. Since Relay no longer needs a license, **you don't need a Relay Account**, and magic-link sign-in will be retired in a future update.

- You can use every part of Relay without ever signing in.
- If you already signed in, nothing breaks. Your dashboards and settings are stored on your Mac, not in your account.
- You can delete your Relay Account any time in `Relay > Settings` → <Icon icon="heart" /> **Support**. Deleting it removes your account data from our servers.
- If you're signed in and have diagnostics sharing on, Relay can send an optional heartbeat. See [Permissions](/relay/account/permissions#usage-diagnostics-and-the-relay-account-heartbeat).

## Troubleshooting

### A feature still looks locked

1. Quit and reopen Relay.
2. Choose `Relay > Check for Updates...` and install the latest version.
3. If it's still locked on `1.3.0` or later, contact `support@ondeckapps.com` with your app version.

### Do I need to sign in?

No. Relay works without an account. If you'd rather remove an account you already created, delete it from `Relay > Settings` → <Icon icon="heart" /> **Support**.

<Warning>Vista support is currently in beta. Test it before you rely on it in a live show.</Warning>

Trigger your Vista lighting and show system from Relay.

## Setup

1. Connect to a supported Vista device.
2. In [Automation View](/relay/dashboards/automation), add the **Vista** action node.

## What it can do

Export cue lists and trigger basic Vista OSC commands directly from Relay.

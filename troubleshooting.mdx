Start with the area that matches your problem. Each link goes to that page's troubleshooting section.

## Quick checks

Most issues come down to one of these:

1. **Permissions.** Confirm Local Network, Microphone, or Speech Recognition access under [Permissions](/account/permissions).
2. **Same network.** Relay and the device or service must be reachable on the same network.
3. **License.** If a feature looks locked, refresh your status under [License](/account/license).
4. **Restart.** After granting a permission, quit and reopen Relay.
